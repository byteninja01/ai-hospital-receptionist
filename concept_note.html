CONCEPT NOTE

Project Title:

MedEye: AI-Powered Hospital Receptionist

1. Background / Context

Hospital front-desks and reception areas are critical touchpoints where delays directly impact patient health outcomes and overall satisfaction. Traditional manual intake processes are often time-consuming, prone to data entry errors, and create a significant administrative burden on medical staff.

Current hospital intake flows suffer from high friction, leading to "waiting room fatigue" and delayed triage for critical cases. MedEye bridges this gap by providing an immediate, intelligent response layer.

2. Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to revolutionize the patient onboarding experience by automating the entire intake and triage lifecycle using agentic AI.

  • Automate patient information gathering via natural, empathetic conversation.
  • Perform real-time medical triage and ward classification to prioritize urgent cases.
  • Ensure 100% structured data capture for seamless EMR/HIS integration.

3. Target Audience / Beneficiaries

MedEye is designed for a multi-layered ecosystem of healthcare stakeholders.

  • Patients: Immediate attention and reduced waiting times.
  • Medical Clinics & Hospitals: Operational efficiency and reduced workload for staff.
  • Triage Clinicians: Reliable, pre-processed patient data for faster decision-making.

4. Key Activities

The implementation follows a modular, engineering-first approach to ensure reliability and security.

  • Graph-Based Logic: Developing a LangGraph workflow for robust multi-step reasoning.
  • Structured Extraction: Implementing Pydantic-powered schemas for accurate data parsing.
  • Triage Engine: Building a classification node that maps symptoms to specific hospital wards.
  • Premium Interface: Crafting a high-performance React front-end with real-time status updates.

5. Expected Outcomes

Successful deployment of MedEye is expected to deliver measurable improvements in hospital operations.

  • 40% reduction in average wait time at the reception.
  • Zero human-entry errors in initial patient intake records.
  • Improved triage accuracy through standardized, LLM-backed medical classification logic.

6. Timeline

Month 1 Research, Graph Logic Development, & LLM Tuning.
Month 2 Frontend Integration, UI Refinement, & Alpha Testing.
Month 3 Pilot Deployment & API Optimization for scale.

8. Conclusion

MedEye represents a paradigm shift in healthcare logistics. By offloading repetitive intake tasks to a secure, intelligent system, we empower medical professionals to focus on what matters most: patient care. This initiative is a scalable solution to a global challenge in medical infrastructure.
